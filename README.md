Two lines explaining a traffic jam to a 10-year-old — a [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna).

## The two lines

> At 5:40 on the toll road, Dad's car is a blob of toothpaste stuck inside the tube while a hundred other blobs press in behind it, none of them able to squeeze out.
>
> The tube fits because the cars really are packed bumper to bumper with nowhere to go, but it leaves out the honking, the lane-changing, and the fact that toothpaste never finds a side road to escape.

363 characters. Line 1 is one vivid sentence with a single analogy — a traffic jam as toothpaste stuck in its tube, anchored at 5:40 on the toll road with Dad's car in it. Line 2 says honestly what the analogy maps (cars packed bumper to bumper with nowhere to go) and what it leaves out (the honking, the lane-changing, and the fact that toothpaste never finds a side road to escape).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the entry is exactly two lines, one sentence each, of 1–400 characters, writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), then reads it back to verify the write.
